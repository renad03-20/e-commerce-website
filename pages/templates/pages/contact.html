Frequently Asked Questions

How do I track my order?

Open My Account and choose Order History. Your order details show the shipping status and available tracking details for each artwork.

What's your return policy?

Buyers can cancel an order before the artist begins preparing it, or a commission before work starts. For a problem after work or delivery, contact support with your order number.

How do I become an artist on your platform?

Sign up for an account and check "I am an artist" during registration. Use your artist dashboard to add artwork and manage orders.

How do I request a custom artwork?

Browse Artists, open a portfolio and choose Request a Commission. The artist must be accepting requests. Review the artist's price before paying.

Where can I check the total?

The cart and checkout show the artwork subtotal, shipping, tax and total before you pay.

What should I include in a support request?

Describe what happened and include the relevant order or commission reference. Do not send passwords or card details.
