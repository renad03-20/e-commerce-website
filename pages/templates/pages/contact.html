Frequently Asked Questions

How do I track my order?

Once your order is shipped, you'll receive a tracking number via email. You can also view it in your order history under "My Account".

What's your return policy?

We offer a 14-day return policy for damaged items or significant discrepancies. Custom artworks may have different return conditions.

How do I become an artist on your platform?

Sign up for an account and check "I am an artist" during registration. You'll need to provide some portfolio samples for verification.

When will I get paid for my artwork sales?

Artists receive payments 7 days after order delivery, to allow for any potential returns or issues.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes! We ship worldwide. Shipping costs and times vary by location and will be calculated at checkout.

How are the artworks packaged?

Artists are required to use professional packaging materials including bubble wrap, corner protectors, and sturdy boxes for safe shipping.
