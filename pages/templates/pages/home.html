Frequently asked questions

Find answers to common questions about purchasing artwork, shipping, and more.

View All

How do I purchase artwork?

Simply browse our gallery, add items to your cart, and proceed to checkout. You'll need to create an account to complete your purchase.

Where can I see shipping costs?

Your cart and checkout show shipping, tax and the final total before you pay.

Can I cancel an order?

You can cancel an order before the artist starts preparing it. For a commission, cancellation is available before work starts. Contact support about later issues.

Can I request a custom artwork?

Browse Artists, open a portfolio and choose Request a Commission when the artist is accepting requests.
